subsection: Storage

- **A grouping or an aggregate over a projected Split model reads the promoted column (#2424).** Filters, sorts and
  member projections written after `Select(r => r.Data)` already reached a `[PhysicalField]` column, but a `GroupBy` key,
  its element and result selectors, an aggregate over a group's members, and `Sum`, `Min`, `Max`, `Average`, `Count`,
  `LongCount`, `Any` or `All` over the projected model read the document. Under `FieldStorageMode.Split` the document
  holds the field's default, so every row fell in one group and sums came back zero while the query succeeded. These
  now read the column, as the row form (`r.Data.Status`) always did. A use of the group itself as a value, or an
  operator over the members whose result is a model (an ordering, `First`), still reads the document for that part.
  Nothing to change; the workaround of projecting the key first is no longer needed.

---
category: Added
subsection: Messaging & delivery
---
- **An operator can exclude received events from integrity checks, and reverse it (#2588).** An escape hatch, never
  a repair: `IIntegrityExclusions` (registered by both Postgres drivers) marks received events by id or by selection
  (origin, types, a time range, or what is currently unresolved before a time), with a reason and who asked, and with
  a dry run. The integrity engine then stops comparing, asking about, holding, healing and alarming on them, on both
  sides of every comparison: the event leaves this service's folds through the eligibility rule and is removed from
  the origin's answer before it is compared, so it never shows as a mismatch, and the origin is told nothing. Only
  received history this service stores can be excluded; its own events are refused (their consumers compare against
  them), and excluding events a service is missing is open question #2616. Larger calls are queued and applied by a
  maintenance step in bounded batches. Every mark and unmark is written to an append-only audit log and logged as a
  Warning per origin; `excluded_events`, `exclusions_pending`, `exclusions_marked` and `exclusions_unmarked` report
  them. Opt-in operator endpoints: `app.MapWhizbangIntegrityExclusionEndpoints()`. New keys under
  `Whizbang:StreamIntegrity:Exclusions` (`BatchSize`, `InlineLimit`, `LogRetention`).
- **The integrity eligibility rule is version 2 (#2588).** It leaves out an excluded event, so a pair of services
  running different versions halts its comparisons until both run version 2: an Error per halted pair with reason
  `eligibility_sql_version`, `pair_halts` and `pair_state`, and the pair-halt alert once it outlasts
  `PairGuard:HaltedAlertAfter`. Services deploy independently, so the pause lasts until both sides of each pair have
  upgraded. Comparing such a pair under the older rule instead is open question #2615. **Upgrade:** expect the halt
  alert during a staggered rollout, or raise `PairGuard:HaltedAlertAfter` for its duration.

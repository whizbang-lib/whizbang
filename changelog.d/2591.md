- **A stream whose ephemeral bodies were reaped no longer fails every perspective batch it lands in (#2591).** Filling a
  received event's missing origin position could reorder an ephemeral stream written long enough ago that the reap had
  deleted its bodies, and the rewind it then queued was a replay of events nothing could read. The perspective drain's
  read threw `InvalidCastException` on the missing body, and the worker lost the whole batch (up to
  `MaxStreamsPerBatch` streams) to that one row, over and over, without ever counting a failure against it. Three
  changes: a rewind now starts no earlier than the first event at or after its trigger that still has a body and that
  the perspective reads, and one with no such event is skipped, so the cursor and its snapshots stay as they are
  (migration 600, `wh_rewind_reap_floor`; counted on `whizbang.stream_integrity.origin_position_rewinds_skipped` and
  logged once per answer); and the same rule repairs what a database already holds, once: a cursor flagged to rewind
  from a trigger with no floor is unflagged, so a startup in Blocking mode is no longer held behind it, one with a floor
  above its trigger is moved there, and their queued rows without a body are deleted (`_wh_repair_unreplayable_rewinds`,
  which raises a NOTICE with the counts). A clamped rewind replays from the snapshot below the floor: the best state
  available, not a full refold of the reaped history. The drain's read returns a row whose body is gone as unreadable,
  naming an ephemeral event's reaped body apart from a Sourced event's missing one (`StreamEventData.UnreadableReason`,
  `BODY_REAPED` or `BODY_MISSING`), instead of throwing, on both the Entity Framework and the Dapper drivers. The worker
  takes such a row out of the batch alone, with the new reason `MessageFailureReason.EventBodyMissing`, and applies the
  rest: a reaped body is dead-lettered on first sight (EventId 84, Warning), since it never comes back, and a Sourced
  body's absence is retried with backoff and dead-lettered past `MaxPerspectiveEventAttempts` (EventId 85, Error). A lost
  drain batch's report now names and counts each stream once. Nothing to change.

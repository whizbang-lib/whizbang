- **A stream whose ephemeral bodies were reaped no longer fails every perspective batch it lands in (#2591).** Filling a
  received event's missing origin position could reorder an ephemeral stream written long enough ago that the reap had
  deleted its bodies, and the rewind it then queued was a replay of events nothing could read. The perspective drain's
  read threw `InvalidCastException` on the missing body, and the worker lost the whole batch (up to
  `MaxStreamsPerBatch` streams) to that one row, over and over, without ever counting a failure against it. Three
  changes: a rewind now starts no earlier than the first event at or after its trigger that still has a body and that
  the perspective reads, and one with no such event is skipped, so the cursor and its snapshots stay as they are
  (migration 600, `wh_rewind_reap_floor`; counted on `whizbang.stream_integrity.origin_position_rewinds_skipped` and
  logged once per answer). The drain's read returns a row whose body is gone as unreadable (`StreamEventData.UnreadableReason`)
  instead of throwing, on both the Entity Framework and the Dapper drivers. And the worker fails such a row alone, with
  the new reason `MessageFailureReason.EventBodyMissing` and a warning naming the perspective, the stream and the event,
  so it is retried with backoff and dead-lettered once its failures pass `MaxPerspectiveEventAttempts`, while the rest
  of the batch is applied. Nothing to change.

- **A sync wait on a perspective covers the rows a foreign event fans out to (#2637).** A wait such as
  `[AwaitPerspectiveSync(typeof(OrderSummaryProjection), EventTypes = [typeof(CustomerRenamed)])]` timed out, because
  the collective sink applied the rename to the order rows but reported it only under its own name. Once a fan-out's
  set-based apply commits, the sink now marks the event applied and processed under each perspective that fans it
  out, once per perspective however many keys it targets by, so the wait completes whether the apply changed one row,
  many or none. A fan-out still fires no lifecycle stage per row.

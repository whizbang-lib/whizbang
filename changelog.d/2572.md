---
category: Fixed
subsection: Storage
---
- **A sealed digest epoch now stays equal to the events it covers when an event lands in it, or moves in or out of it,
  after it was sealed (#2572).** A backfill or redelivery answer stored by the receive path at its origin's position
  could fall in an epoch sealed long before it arrived, and nothing refolded that epoch. Its fold then counted fewer
  events than the store held, the windowed audit against the origin mismatched on every pass, the stream drill-down
  found the live rows equal and opened nothing, and the seal never advanced past the first such epoch (so, with
  `integrity_cut_waits_for_seal` on, archiving waited forever). The event store now records every sealed epoch a write
  changes, by insert or by a change of an event's origin or origin position, whichever path made it, and refolds each
  one once at commit, only when its fold no longer matches, under a per-epoch lock. A rewrite of this service's own
  sealed history also moves its origin generation, as the explicit rewrite paths already did. Epochs that drifted
  before this fix are healed once, with nothing to turn on: on upgrade every sealed epoch of each received lane is
  queued, and each maintenance closure pass re-checks the lowest 8 of them (`integrity_epoch_heal_per_cycle` in
  `wh_settings`) and refolds those that drifted; `integrity_epoch_heal_refolded` counts the refolds and the queue
  (`wh_digest_epoch_heal_queue`) is empty when the heal is done. The heal runs where sealing runs
  (`Whizbang:StreamIntegrity:Sealing:Enabled`, on by default).

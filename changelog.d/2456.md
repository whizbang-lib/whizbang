- **Every ephemeral perspective keeps a rewind floor at the reap boundary (#2456).** Following #2296, two shapes still
  left a late event inside the grace window with no snapshot below it. A model with collectives was snapshotted at the
  cursor: the reap-boundary fold now folds the row's collectives too, those placed before the boundary's last event,
  each after every stream event it was committed after, and the snapshot records the boundary's commit sequence as its
  collectives mark; a collective still in flight defers it. And a snapshot taken at the tip during normal processing
  replaced the boundary snapshot in the single slot and still satisfied the reap's coverage gate: an ephemeral
  perspective's live and rewind cadences now fill an empty slot but never replace the snapshot there, the reap-boundary
  bootstrap takes the slot (older snapshots are pruned), and the reap counts a snapshot as covering only once its own
  event is past the grace window (migration 534). Until then the bodies below a tip snapshot stay and the pair is a
  reap-driven target. One snapshot slot per stream, as before. Nothing to change.

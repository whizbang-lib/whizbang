- **A rebuild folds each collective after every event it was committed after, even when a late event replays at its
  origin (#2449).** A late-arriving event replays at its origin position, so a stream's replay order does not always
  rise with its commit sequence. The rebuild put a collective before the first stream event committed after it, so a
  late event pulled every collective committed before its arrival ahead of the older events replaying after it, which
  overwrote the collective's change while the rebuild reported success. A collective now goes after every stream event
  it was committed after, wherever the replay places that event. Nothing to change; rebuild a perspective that has
  collective applies and late-arriving events to correct rows written by an earlier rebuild.

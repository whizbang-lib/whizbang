- **An event a service wrote keeps no origin, even when its type lives in another service's domain (#2573).** The
  slim stored form records no service on the event's first hop, so the stream-integrity passes that give history
  received before origins were recorded its origin read a service's own events as received ones: a receptor's
  reaction of a type owned by the service that sent the trigger was re-attributed to that service with no origin
  position (which then answered "unknown" when asked for it), and a service's own event carried back in another
  service's redelivery bundle adopted that bundle's origin. The first hop's service is now read from the stored row,
  the event's route or its staged full form, and an event written after origins were recorded whose first hop names
  no service anywhere stays local. Migration 594. **Upgrade:** nothing to change.
- **Events a service wrote that were already stored under another origin return to the local lane (#2573).** A
  one-off maintenance step (`written-here-origin-restoration`) walks the event store once, in bounded idle-gated
  batches, and returns every row recorded under another origin whose first hop names this service: a reaction moved
  to the trigger's owner with no position, and an own event that adopted another service's redelivery origin. Its
  digest moves back to the local lane, the sealed epochs it left and joined are refolded, its origin's "unknown"
  answer and held count go, and the repair-ledger buckets it touched start afresh. A row nothing proves was written
  here is left and counted (`whizbang.stream_integrity.written_here_restoration_ambiguous`): a slim row whose first
  hop names no service anywhere cannot be told from a received one. It needs the service's name, so it runs as a
  maintenance step rather than in the migration; it is off with `Whizbang:StreamIntegrity:ReattributionEnabled`.
  Migration 599. **Upgrade:** nothing to change.

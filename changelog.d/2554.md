- **With ownership by map on, every instance claims against the one published map, and none ranks itself (#2554).** A
  claimer with no valid map claims nothing and waits, instead of ranking the live instances itself, so two claimers can
  never use two maps at once. A map stays valid while its lease runs, and past its lease while every one of its members
  is live, so an assigner that lapses never stalls claims; the claim says when it waits
  (`whizbang.partition_map_unavailable`). The wake-up notification goes to the instance the map names for each stream's
  partition, with the switch off as well: a stream no instance holds wakes its partition's owner in the published map
  while that owner is live, and the live list's residue only when the map names no live owner. With the switch off,
  claims rank as before, by the published map's share (#2558). Migrations 565 and 566.

- **Every test fixture tears down once, and the build keeps it that way (#2468).** Contributor-facing only: the Dapper,
  SQLite and RabbitMQ integration fixtures (41 classes, `PostgresTestBase` and `DapperTestBase` among them) were both
  disposable and had an `[After(Test)]` hook, so TUnit ran their teardown twice. Each keeps the hook as its one teardown
  and drops the interface. A new repository-internal analyzer rule, **WHIZ609**, reports a class that is both as a build
  error in every test project, in place of a guard test per project. Nothing to change for consumers of the library.

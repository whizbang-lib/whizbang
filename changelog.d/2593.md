- **A role lapses only when another live instance can take it (#2593).** A sole instance whose holder loop stalls past
  its progress window keeps its roles and their epochs instead of losing them and winning them back after a cool-down;
  the cool-down is answered only while a peer that could take the role exists. In a fleet of two or more, a live peer
  that votes still takes over a stuck holder at the next epoch, and a holder the liveness rule calls dead still lapses
  at its lease. The `roles` health component reports a stalled sole holder Degraded ("stalled with no peer to take
  over"), `wh_role_assignment_status()` gains a `stalled_without_successor` column, and the
  `whizbang.roles.stalled_without_successor` meter counts each read that found one. A healthy sole holder reads
  Operational: having no peer is not a fault. **Upgrade:** nothing to change; alert on the new health detail or meter
  if a single-instance service should be restarted when its holder stalls.

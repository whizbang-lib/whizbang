- **An opt-in write-behind perspective write mode, for read models with a few very hot streams (#2247).**
  `PerspectiveWriteMode.WriteBehind` (service-wide through `PerspectiveWorkerOptions.WriteMode`, or per perspective
  with `[PerspectiveWriteMode(PerspectiveWriteMode.WriteBehind)]`) holds each folded model in memory across drains and
  writes it later, with the same set-based writer, so a stream's many small batches cost one write. It is never the
  default. Its cache is bounded: `Whizbang:Workers:Perspective:WriteBehind:MaxModels` and `MaxBytes` are required, and
  a worker that writes anything behind without them does not start; `MaxFlushIntervalMilliseconds` (default 1,000, at
  most half of the lease less its grace) and `MaxBatchesPerModel` (default 32) bound how long and how much a model is
  held. A held batch's checkpoint, sync signal and post-perspective stages wait for its flush; a sync wait on a held
  event, a rewind, a claimed or per-pair apply of the same stream, and the worker stopping flush it at once; a crash
  loses only the held models, which replay from their checkpoints; a model whose lease ran out is dropped, never
  written; and a writer outside the runner that changed the row meanwhile is met by the version check of the merged
  write, which re-applies each held batch on the row it finds. New metrics:
  `whizbang.perspective.write_behind.models`, `.bytes`, `.lookups` (hit rate), `.flush_lag`, `.forced_flushes` and
  `.dropped`. `PerspectiveWorker.FlushWriteBehindAsync` flushes every held model on demand.

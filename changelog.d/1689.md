category: Changed
subsection: Observability & tooling
---
- **The perspective runner generator processes its large template once per build, not once per perspective (#1689).**
  It copied and edited about 190 KB of template text for every perspective, which took about 32 seconds on the
  build-cost benchmark's 1,000-perspective consumer. The shared runner logic is now emitted once per assembly (#2401),
  the per-runner template and header are read once per run, and the generator's time on the same consumer drops to a
  fraction of a second. Nothing to change; a large consumer builds faster.

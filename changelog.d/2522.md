---
category: Changed
subsection: Rebuilds
---
- **A rebuild in place writes its rows a batch at a time (#2522).** An in-place rebuild and a rebuild of named streams
  now write the rows of each batch of streams they read together through the driver's write sink, the set-based write
  the perspective worker already uses (prepare, one write per table and shape, complete), instead of one statement per
  row. Its statement count therefore grows with its batches, not its streams, as a blue-green rebuild's already does.
  Each row ends exactly as before: the same fold, stamp, cursor, counts and progress; a write the ordering guard
  refuses is still reported already correct or refused and never rewound; a row that moves under the batch's write is
  read again and written on its own; a purge is recorded before the batch removes the row; events an upcaster re-keyed
  onto another row are folded with that row after the batch, as before. It follows the service's perspective write
  mode: a service or perspective that writes per stream (`PerspectiveWorkerOptions.WriteMode`, or
  `[PerspectiveWriteMode(PerStream)]`), a host whose driver registers no write sink (the Dapper driver), and a stream
  read on its own (`StreamReadBatchSize` 1, archived data) keep writing each row on its own. No migration.

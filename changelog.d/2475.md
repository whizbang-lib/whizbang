category: Changed
---
- **The Aspire hosting packages move to Aspire 17, and net11.0 builds take the Microsoft.Extensions 11 line
  (#2475).** `Whizbang.Hosting.Azure.ServiceBus` and `Whizbang.Hosting.RabbitMQ` now depend on Aspire.Hosting
  17.0.0 (was 13.6.5). Where a package depends on Microsoft.Extensions.Hosting, DependencyInjection,
  Diagnostics.HealthChecks or FileSystemGlobbing, its net11.0 dependency group now floors them at 11.0.0,
  which Aspire 17 requires on net11.0. The net10.0 group is unchanged and still floors them at 10.0.15, so a
  net10.0 (LTS) app is not moved onto Microsoft.Extensions 11. **Upgrade:** an AppHost on Aspire 13 moves to
  Aspire 17 (`Aspire.AppHost.Sdk/17.0.0`) before taking this release; `AddProject` is obsolete there in favor
  of `AddDotnetProject` from `Aspire.Hosting.Dotnet`, and `Aspire.Hosting.NodeJs` is replaced by
  `Aspire.Hosting.JavaScript`.

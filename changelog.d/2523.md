- **A claim of perspective work costs the same at every stream depth (#2523).** Each stream the claim selects is walked
  in event order up to the claim's row bound, with a check per event that no earlier event of the stream is still
  leased or scheduled. The planner expected that check to reject nearly every row, so for streams it judged short it read
  the stream's whole pending tail, sorted it, and ran the check on every row, each check reading every earlier event. A
  claim over streams 25, 50 and 100 events deep read 2,998, 8,561 and 3,541 blocks, against about 1,400 at 12 and at 200.
  The check is now written so the planner expects it to pass, the walk stops at the row bound at every depth, and the
  same claims read 1,347, 1,397 and 1,111 blocks. The events a claim takes, and their order, are unchanged. Nothing to
  change.

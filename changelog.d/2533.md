---
category: Changed
subsection: Rebuilds
---
- **A rebuild flushes the write-behind models of the perspective it rebuilds before it reads a row (#2533).** A
  model a write-behind perspective held before a rebuild started is now written first, every model of the
  perspective for a rebuild of every stream and only the named streams' models for a rebuild of named streams,
  instead of being written after the rebuild and met by its row-version check. Nothing to change; the flush is
  counted under the new `rebuild` reason of `whizbang.perspective.write_behind.forced_flushes`.

- **The sample Service Bus lifecycle tests assert that each perspective stage fires once per event per perspective
  (#2581).** The RabbitMQ and Service Bus sample suites now share one lifecycle stage helper, which waits for a
  perspective stage once for each perspective that folds the event, and the Service Bus hosts register the firing
  observer it waits on. Nothing to change.

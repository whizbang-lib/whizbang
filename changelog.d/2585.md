---
category: Changed
subsection: Storage
---
- **The framework's rewind and rebuild events are kept for 14 days by default, down from 30 (#2585).** The default of
  `Whizbang:Ephemeral:SystemEvents:Ttl` is now `14.00:00:00`, both for the TTL stamped on a new event and for the
  grace the reaper measures from each stored event's creation time, so stored rewind and rebuild events older than
  14 days are reaped after the upgrade. **Upgrade:** to keep the old retention, set
  `Whizbang:Ephemeral:SystemEvents:Ttl` to `30.00:00:00`.

category: Changed
subsection: Work coordination, perspectives & sagas
---
- **The published partition map now owns every stream by default, and a rebalance hands a moved partition over instead
  of moving it under a running owner (#2557).** `PartitionAssigner.OwnershipByMap` defaults on and binds from
  `Whizbang:Database:PartitionAssigner:OwnershipByMap`. The assigner still turns a service's switch on only once every
  live instance of that service supports the mode, so a rolling deploy of mixed releases keeps the stored-owner rules
  until the last old instance is gone. In map mode, a publish that moves a partition away from a live owner sets it
  handing over, with the next owner and a deadline (`Whizbang:Database:PartitionAssigner:HandoverDeadline`, default 30
  seconds, judged on the database's clock); nobody claims it meanwhile. The old owner's handover worker waits until no
  stream of the partition is being worked, saves its write-behind models there (flush reason `handover`), lets its queued
  writes land, and acknowledges, which releases its unstarted rows and moves the partition to the next owner at a raised
  epoch. Past the deadline the assigner completes the move itself and releases what the old owner held; the old owner's
  late writes are then fenced (#2556) and its held models dropped, not written. A stopping instance hands its partitions
  over before it deregisters (graceful leave), and a lease renewal no longer keeps a row of a partition the instance no
  longer owns. Every flip takes its ownership rows in partition order, as every write that holds several partitions
  does, so a flip waits for a write and never deadlocks against it. Migrations 571 and 581. **Upgrade:** nothing to configure; set `Whizbang:Database:PartitionAssigner:OwnershipByMap`
  to `false` to keep the stored-owner rules. An implementation of `IPartitionAssignmentStore` takes the handover deadline
  in `PublishAsync` and implements `CompleteExpiredHandoversAsync`; `IWorkCoordinator` gains
  `GetPartitionsHandingOverAsync`, `AcknowledgeHandoverAsync` and `GetLeaveStatusAsync`, whose defaults hand nothing over.

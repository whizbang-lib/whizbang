- **A rebalance hands a moved partition over instead of moving it under a running owner (#2557).** With ownership by
  map on, a publish that moves a partition away from a live owner sets it handing over, with the next owner and a
  deadline (`Whizbang:Database:PartitionAssigner:HandoverDeadline`, default 30 seconds, judged on the database's
  clock). Nobody claims a partition while it hands over. Its old owner acknowledges once it has finished, which
  releases its unstarted rows there (attempts refunded) and moves the partition to the next owner at a raised epoch in
  one statement fenced on the old epoch. Past the deadline the assigner completes the move itself and releases what the
  old owner held, whose late writes are then fenced (#2556). A partition whose owner is not live moves at once, and a
  lease renewal no longer keeps a row of a partition the instance no longer owns. With the switch off, a publish moves a
  partition at once, as before. Migration 571. **Upgrade:** an implementation of `IPartitionAssignmentStore` takes the
  handover deadline in `PublishAsync` and implements `CompleteExpiredHandoversAsync`.

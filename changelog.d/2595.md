- **Each instance's assignment is one readable fact, on both Postgres drivers (#2595).** `wh_instance_assignments()`
  derives, per registered instance, its valid roles with their epochs, its share of the partitions, the published map's
  revision and its kind: `starting`, `standard-worker`, `role-holder`, `awaiting-share`, `draining`, `ineligible` or
  `evicted`. Nothing new is stored: moving a partition or a role changes the answer. `IInstanceAssignmentReader` reads
  it, `FleetInstanceStatus` gains `Assignment` and `OwnedPartitions`, and the Dapper driver now registers the fleet
  status source it lacked, through the same reader as the EF Core driver. Readiness does not wait for a share;
  `awaiting-share` is how a missing one is seen.
- **Each instance logs and reports what it was given (#2595).** From the Coordinate stage on, every Postgres instance
  reads its own assignment every `ObserveInterval` (default 30 seconds) and logs `Assigned: ...` when it first appears
  and on every change ("Assigned: role holder (partition-assigner), 2500 partitions, map 12"). A new `assignment`
  health component reports it: an instance awaiting its share is Operational inside `AwaitingShareGrace` and Degraded
  past it (default: two partition-assigner settle windows plus one role renew interval, 15 seconds); a fresh instance not yet
  observed reads Operational too, and Degraded only if no read has succeeded by the end of the grace from the
  monitor's start; after a successful read, a failed read, an instance not in the fleet and an evicted one are Degraded;
  never Faulted. Both options bind from
  `Whizbang:Database:InstanceAssignment`. Readiness still does not wait for a share.

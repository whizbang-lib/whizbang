- **A claim re-offering the streams an instance holds no longer reads every live lease once per stream (#2474).**
  When the statistics were taken while every lease had lapsed (a fan-out that outlasted its leases leaves them so),
  the claim that re-acquired the work read the lease index once per held stream: 1,001,001 index entries for 1,000
  held perspective streams, a cost that grew with the square of the batch. The re-offer now reads one entry per held
  stream on any statistics, for perspective and inbox work alike. Results are unchanged; nothing to change.

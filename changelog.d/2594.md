subsection: Startup

- **Every role is declared once, and an undeclared role is refused (#2594).** The framework's roles (the migrator, the
  maintainer, the commit-order stamper, the partition assigner and the perspective-rebuild family) are declared in the
  new `RoleCatalog`; a host declares its own with `services.AddWhizbangRole(name, RoleKind.Standing or Episodic)`, or a
  family with `AddWhizbangRolePrefix`. A standing role declared this way is also held by the holder loop and reported by
  the `roles` health component. The Postgres role elector refuses a role nothing declared (`DutyRefusal.Refused`, naming
  `AddWhizbangRole`) before any statement runs, and a guard test fails any acquire call in the framework that names an
  undeclared role. **Upgrade:** a startup step whose `RequiredCapability` is a duty of your own, or code that calls
  `IDutyElector.TryAcquireAsync` with your own role name, declares that role with `AddWhizbangRole`; a role already
  listed in `RoleAssignmentOptions.Roles` counts as declared.
  `RoleAssignmentOptions.IsEpisodic` is now an instance member that reads the catalog (`RoleAssignmentOptions.Catalog`,
  filled from the registered catalog), so a role declared episodic is held only by the work that asks for it; call it
  on the options value instead of the type. Leases are unchanged: the migrator alone keeps its longer default lease.

- **Children of a coalesced composite carry their own origin commit sequence (#1628).** A coalesce fold bundles
  singles that were already stored as events, but every child the receiver fanned out copied the composite's own
  sequence, which is zero because a composite is never stored as an event. Origin-order replay then fell back to stored
  order for any stream holding such a child, and integrity accounting never counted one. The fold now reads each
  single's position in the producer's commit lane and `CoalescedEventsComposite` carries it per child
  (`InnerCommitSequences`), so each child carries the sequence a direct delivery of its single would have carried. A
  single with no position (not stored as an event, not yet stamped, or forwarded from another service) carries the
  composite's sequence as before, and so does every child of a composite from an older producer. The field is
  additive on the wire: an older receiver ignores it. Audit-record composites are unchanged, since audit records are
  not stored as events on the producer. Nothing to change.

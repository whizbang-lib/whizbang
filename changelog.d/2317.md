- **The repair gauge is named for what it measures: `whizbang.stream_integrity.repair_close_ratio_percent` (#2317).**
  The gauge reports `100 * closed / opened` over the rolling window, a ratio of two rates that says whether the
  integrity backlog shrinks (above 100) or grows (below 100), not how much of the repair is done. It was named
  `repair_progress_percent`, which read as "nearly done" at 87% and as a bug at 560%. The formula, unit and "no
  measurement when nothing opened" rule are unchanged. How much is left, and when it will be done, are
  `ledger_open_buckets` and `backlog_eta_seconds`. Its setting is now `Whizbang:StreamIntegrity:RepairCloseRatioGaugeEnabled`,
  and the window it shares with the ETA is `Whizbang:StreamIntegrity:CompletionWindowMinutes`. For one release the
  gauge is also reported under its old name with the same value, and the old keys `RepairProgressGaugeEnabled` and
  `RepairProgressWindowMinutes` still bind (a new key wins when both are set); the C# properties of those names are
  `[Obsolete]`. In code, `RepairProgressReading` is now `RepairRatesReading` (its `ProgressPercent` is
  `CloseRatioPercent`), `StreamIntegrityMetrics.UpdateRepairProgress` is `UpdateRepairRates`, and
  `IntegrityCompletion.RepairProgressPercent` is `RepairCloseRatioPercent`. **Upgrade:** point dashboards and alerts at
  `repair_close_ratio_percent` and read it as a direction signal, rename the two configuration keys, and rename any of
  the C# members above you call; the old metric name and keys are removed in the next release.

category: Changed
subsection: Messaging & delivery
---
- **An inbox row waiting for its stream's head says so (#1630).** A received event that sorts after an earlier event
  of its stream that is not yet stored (and that this instance does not hold) is held back, unstored and undispatched,
  so the stream is stored in the producer's order. Such a row used to read as a fresh row, and once its lease ran out
  and another claim took it, as a dispatch cut off ("ended without a reported outcome ... process terminated
  mid-dispatch", `LeaseExpired`), although nothing was dispatched. It now records
  `MessageFailureReason.AwaitingStreamHead` (30) in `wh_inbox_state.failure_reason`, with an error naming the stream
  and when the wait began; the state is cleared when the row is stored, and the wait is still not charged an attempt.
  A lapse during the wait is no longer recorded as `LeaseExpired`, and a row that carried that record from an earlier
  release while waiting shows the wait instead. Operators can list waiting rows with
  `SELECT stream_id, error FROM wh_inbox_state WHERE failure_reason = 30`. Nothing to change in consumer code; a
  dashboard or query that groups inbox rows by failure reason gains the new value.

- **A rewind from a snapshot at the stream's highest id now writes its row for a versioned-apply perspective
  (#2519).** When a stream held a newer event with the stream's highest id and a snapshot there, and events stored
  after it carried lower ids (events a retried handling returns derive their ids from that older handling), a rewind for
  them restored the snapshot, folded them and moved the cursor, but the row of an `IVersionedApplyTarget` model kept the
  snapshot's state: the replay stamped its write with the highest id it had read after the snapshot, below the id the
  row was stamped at, and the versioned-apply guard refused it. The replay's frontier now starts at the snapshot's event,
  so the write lands and the row is the fold in stream order. Nothing to change; a row left behind this way is corrected
  by the next rewind of its stream, or by a rebuild of the perspective.

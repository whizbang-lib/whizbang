category: Removed
subsection: Work coordination, perspectives & sagas

- **Unused coordination tables and their readers are gone.** `wh_partition_assignments` had no reader or writer: the
  bootstrap no longer creates it and migration 476 drops it. `wh_receptor_processing` had no writer, yet the claim
  probed it on every poll; the claim, the instance release paths, the stream purge (which no longer reports a
  `wh_receptor_processing` row), the queue-statistics refresh and the rewind cleanup no longer touch it, and
  `claim_orphaned_receptor_work` is dropped. The table itself stays, empty, for migration 031.
- **Public API: the types of the unused tables are removed.** `PartitionAssignmentsSchema` declared the dropped table;
  `ReceptorProcessingRecord`, `ReceptorProcessingStatus`, `ReceptorProcessingCompletion` and
  `ReceptorProcessingFailure` described rows nothing wrote and were accepted by no coordinator method. **Upgrade:** delete any reference to them; there is no replacement.

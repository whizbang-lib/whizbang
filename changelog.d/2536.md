- **A set-based perspective batch whose lease runs out now ends every pair that had already ended with its own
  outcome (#2536).** A pair that had produced its result is reported with it, and a pair that had failed is reported with
  its own failure, as the per-pair path ends an apply that returned. Before, such a pair was neither reported nor failed,
  and its rows replayed once the lease lapsed. Pairs still in flight when the lease runs out are failed on the lease, as
  before.

- **Events choose their sequence integrity: publish at once, hold briefly for the commit-order stamp, or hold until
  stamped (#2542).** A receiver now stores an event with its origin position instead of none whenever the origin
  holds it for its stamp. Set it per dispatch on `DispatchOptions`, per event type with
  `[SequenceIntegrity(mode, holdMilliseconds)]`, or for the service with `Whizbang:Core:SequenceIntegrity` and
  `Whizbang:Core:SequenceHoldTime`. The default is `Bounded` with a 1 second hold, released as soon as the stamp lands;
  a stream with a held event holds its later events too. `None` keeps the previous behavior. The gauge
  `whizbang.outbox.sequence_hold.oldest_age_seconds` reports the oldest held event. **Upgrade:** published events may
  now leave up to the hold time later when the stamper is behind; set `SequenceIntegrity` to `None` on a type, a
  dispatch or the service to keep publishing at once.

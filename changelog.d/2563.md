- **The Dapper driver reports the Converge startup stage, as the EF Core driver does (#2563).** A start on the Dapper
  driver now enters Converge just before it reconciles the managed schema objects, on a fresh or a current schema, so a
  host or an `IStartupStageReporter` watching the stages sees the same sequence on either driver. Nothing to change.

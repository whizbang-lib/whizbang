category: Fixed
subsection: Messaging & delivery
---
- **A PreDistributeInline failure on an interval or batch flush no longer loses the other callers' messages (#2313).**
  Those flushes carry the queued messages of many callers and clear the queue before they run. A `PreDistributeInline`
  receptor that threw for one message stopped the flush before the store, so every message in it was lost. Now only
  the message a receptor failed for is refused: it is not stored and its failure is logged against it, every other
  message is stored and distributed, and the flush throws to no caller, since it belongs to none. The immediate and
  scoped strategies are unchanged: their flush carries only the caller's own messages, and a failure still stops it
  and is thrown to that caller. Nothing to change.

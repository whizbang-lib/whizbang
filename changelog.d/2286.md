- **The perspective work-queue calls no longer slow down as the backlog and the applied history grow (#2286).**
  Planned for a batch of 1,000 streams, the claim's re-acquisition of orphaned rows, its ledger write, the drain's
  fetch, the completion flush and the stream eviction each read a whole table on every call (the stream ledger, the
  pending work rows, the event store or every cursor), so a fan-out to 100,000 new streams applied about five times
  slower per stream than one to 10,000. Each now reaches the other table one key at a time, so a call reads in
  proportion to its batch: for the same batch over 40,000 pending and 40,000 applied streams, the acquisition reads
  22,001 rows instead of 212,011, the fetch 8,000 instead of 87,022, the completion 10,013 instead of 89,017 and
  the eviction 2,001 instead of 39,004. Nothing to change; the next startup's migration applies the new SQL.

- **A replay on the Dapper driver now folds a received stream in its origin's order (#2509).** The Dapper event store's
  reads left every event's origin (`origin_service_id`, `origin_commit_sequence`) off its envelope, so a rewind or a
  rebuild treated every received event as local and folded the stream in arrival order, where the EF Core driver dealt
  it into the order the origin committed it in. An update the origin committed before a delete could replay after it,
  and the row end holding the older value. Every Dapper read that materializes events (both `ReadAsync` overloads,
  `ReadPolymorphicAsync`, `GetEventsBetweenAsync` and `GetEventsBetweenPolymorphicAsync`) now carries the origin, and
  `JsonbPersistenceModel` gains `OriginServiceId` and `OriginCommitSequence` for the envelope rebuilt from a row.

category: Added
subsection: Rebuilds

- **A blue-green rebuild now loads its shadow table with `COPY` and builds its plain indexes after the load (#2248).**
  The first pass of every blue-green rebuild writes an empty table that only the rebuild writes, so its rows are now
  held and copied in chunks (1,000 rows by default, `BlueGreenRebuildOptions.StagingChunkRows`) instead of upserted one
  at a time, a row the rebuild has not written is known absent without a read, and the shadow's indexes that no key,
  uniqueness rule or constraint depends on are dropped for the load and built once every row is in. The catch-up of
  writes committed during the rebuild, and the swap, are unchanged and run the per-row write with every index in place.
  A row the table refuses fails only its own stream, as before: its cursor is not written and the rebuild reports it.
  It is chosen for every blue-green rebuild; a service can switch it off with `BlueGreenRebuildOptions.StagingLoad =
  false`. Rebuilds of selected streams are in place and unaffected. The swap now also gives the installed table the
  live table's owner and exactly its grants, which `CREATE TABLE … (LIKE … INCLUDING ALL)` never copied, so a role
  granted access to a perspective table keeps it across a rebuild. Nothing to change.

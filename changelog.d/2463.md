category: Changed
subsection: Storage
---
- **A perspective mixing ephemeral and durable events keeps a tip snapshot beside its reap-boundary floor (#2463).**
  Since #2456 an ephemeral perspective's one snapshot is its floor, moved only by the reap-boundary bootstrap, so once
  the floor was taken a mixed perspective got no fresh snapshot and a rewind replayed every durable event since the last
  reap boundary. Such a perspective now keeps two kinds of snapshot. The floor is unchanged: only the reap-boundary
  bootstrap writes or moves it, and it is folded from the floor below, never from a tip. The tip is written by the live
  and rewind cadences, one per stream, kept only above the newest floor, and never counts as a floor for the reap, the
  reap-driven snapshot targets or the destruction batch. A rewind restores the newest snapshot before its late event: the
  tip when it is there and can be restored, otherwise the floor. A late event removes the tip at or above it, which was
  taken without it, both when a cursor is flagged for it (a trigger, so every path and every instance version drops it)
  and in the rewind itself. An eviction keeps the latest floor and the latest tip; a purge, a truncate and the other
  removers take tips with the floors. Perspectives that apply only ephemeral or only durable events are unchanged.
  Migration 620 adds `wh_perspective_snapshots.is_tip` (false for every existing snapshot) and redefines
  `wh_perform_maintenance` and `_wh_evict_perspective_row_companions`. `IPerspectiveSnapshotStore` gains
  `CreateTipSnapshotAsync`, `GetFloorSnapshotAsync` and `DeleteTipSnapshotFromAsync`, with defaults that keep no tips, and
  `PerspectiveSnapshotFloor.IsTip`. **Upgrade:** nothing to change. A custom snapshot store keeps working and keeps no
  tips; to give mixed perspectives a tip, implement the three members as the Postgres stores do.

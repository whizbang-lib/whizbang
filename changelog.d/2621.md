- **A rebuild or rewind of a row another stream fans out to comes out as the live row did (#2621).** When a row's fan-out
  key takes a stream (the event that sets `CustomerId`, or the snapshot a rewind starts from), the replay reads that
  stream's fan-out events by its id and folds those committed after that point, merged with the row's own events in
  commit order, and only while the key still holds that stream: a rename committed before `OrderPlaced` reaches no row,
  an order moved to another customer takes only the new customer's later renames, and a received stream's renames fold
  in their producer's order (`ReplayEventOrder`). A foreign stream archived through a prefix or whole is read as the
  rebuild reads any stream (`RebuildArchivedData`), a rewind from a snapshot never folds again what the snapshot holds,
  and the reap-boundary snapshot of an ephemeral perspective holds the fan-outs before its boundary. A fan-out still
  being applied live folds only into the rows the sink reached, as a collective does. Nothing is registered by hand:
  the generated fan-out entry gives its model the replay's in-memory executor. Adding a fan-out to a deployed
  perspective rebuilds nothing: the rows it targets take the foreign events committed from then on, and a rebuild or
  any rewind of a row folds the earlier ones.

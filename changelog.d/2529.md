subsection: Storage

- **Archive and prefix truncate no longer wait forever on received history with no origin position (#2529).** With
  `Sealing:Enabled` on, a stream holding a received event whose origin commit sequence was never recorded answered
  `awaiting_seal` on every pass and could never be archived or truncated. Such an event is counted only in its origin's
  open window and no seal ever covers it, so it no longer holds a cut (migration 557); a cut carries it, and the
  origin's answer is unchanged. A received event with a position above the seal, and a local event not yet stamped,
  still hold the cut. Streams held this way are archived on the next end-of-life cycle. Nothing to change.
- **A whole-stream archive the seal holds is counted in `eligible_not_yet_deleted` (#2529).** Only a prefix truncate
  recorded `awaiting_seal`, so an end-of-life archive held by the seal was invisible. It is now counted under task
  `archive`, and forgotten once the archive answers otherwise. Nothing to change.

- **The stream lifecycle cost scenario's vacuum marks every page it measures on a busy server (#2521).** A row's commit
  is hinted only once the WAL is flushed through the commit LSN the commit log keeps for its group of 32 transaction
  ids, the latest commit in the group, and other databases' asynchronous commits landed in the rows' group after the
  scenario flushed; a plain vacuum also passes by a page another backend has pinned for the moment. Either left pages
  unmarked. The scenario now waits until every id of the rows' groups has finished, flushes the WAL once more, and
  vacuums with `FREEZE`, which waits for a pin. Nothing to change.
- **The claim poll cost scenario measures one fixed workload instead of a random draw (#2521).** Its fill drew stream,
  event and poller ids at random, and the ids decide the partitions, so the cost it reported was partly the draw: 7,624
  to 8,125 blocks a call at depth 50 against a ceiling of 8,000. The workload is now fixed, as the dearest id set
  measured (8,563 at depth 50), and its depth-50 ceiling is 10,000 until a mid-depth poll reading five to six times a
  deep one is fixed (#2523). Nothing to change.

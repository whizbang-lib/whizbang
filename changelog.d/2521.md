- **The stream lifecycle cost scenario's vacuum no longer passes a pinned page by (#2521).** A plain vacuum that cannot
  take a page's cleanup lock while another backend holds a pin on it (the checkpointer or background writer writing it,
  a reader with a newer snapshot) prunes the page without marking it all-visible, so on a busy server the scenario
  found pages unmarked. The scenario now vacuums with `FREEZE`, which waits for the pin and marks every page. Nothing
  to change.
- **The claim poll cost scenario measures one fixed workload instead of a random draw (#2521).** Its fill drew stream,
  event and poller ids at random, and the ids decide the partitions, so the cost it reported was partly the draw: 7,624
  to 8,125 blocks a call at depth 50 against a ceiling of 8,000. The workload is now fixed, as the dearest id set
  measured (8,563 at depth 50), and its depth-50 ceiling is 10,000 until a mid-depth poll reading five to six times a
  deep one is fixed (#2523). Nothing to change.

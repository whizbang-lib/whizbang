- **The stream lifecycle cost scenario's vacuum no longer passes a pinned page by (#2521).** A plain vacuum that cannot
  take a page's cleanup lock while another backend holds a pin on it (the checkpointer or background writer writing it,
  a reader with a newer snapshot) prunes the page without marking it all-visible, so on a busy server the scenario
  found pages unmarked. The scenario now vacuums with `FREEZE`, which waits for the pin and marks every page. Nothing
  to change.

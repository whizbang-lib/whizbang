- **A released perspective stream goes to the live instance its partition is assigned to, not back to the one that
  released it regardless (#2545).** Releasing unstarted work (a stuck claim loop's release, the held-work release when
  an instance pauses, stands down or stops, and the perspective worker's release of a failed batch) ended only the
  lease on the stream's ownership, and perspective acquisition lets a live owner keep its streams whatever that lease
  says. So a released perspective stream went straight back to the instance that released it, and a pausing or stopping
  instance's perspective streams waited instead of moving to a peer. The release now clears the stream's stored owner,
  as deregistering does, so the instance its partition is assigned to claims it at its next poll. A released stream
  whose partition is assigned to the releaser still comes back to it, with its attempt refunded. Migration 559.

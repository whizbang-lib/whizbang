- **An idle perspective worker no longer grows its memory without bound (#2613).** Every pass of the perspective
  consumer loop raced a fresh wait on the work channel and the drain channel against its idle timeout and the wake
  signal. A pass that the timeout or the signal ended left both waits behind, and the channels keep a waiting reader
  until an item is written or the host stops, so an instance with no perspective work queued two more waiters each
  pass: hundreds of thousands a day, several megabytes an hour per idle service, until the next restart. The loop now
  keeps one pending wait per channel across passes and asks for a new one only once it has completed, so it holds at
  most one waiter per channel (one per consumer when `MaxConcurrentDrainConsumers` is above one). The two batching
  windows that race a channel wait against a deadline already canceled their wait when the deadline won; tests now
  hold them to it, and a repository analyzer (WHIZ611) fails the build on a channel wait raced in `Task.WhenAny` that
  nothing releases. Nothing to change.
